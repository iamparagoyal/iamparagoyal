A full-stack meeting workspace for exploring transcripts, summaries, action items, and meeting insights.

**Highlights**
- 📝 Transcript viewer with synchronized timestamps and search.
- ✅ Action-item management and meeting summaries.

- 🤖 Optional AI-powered notes and question answering.
